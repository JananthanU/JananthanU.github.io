Causal inference

Right heart catheterization and ICU mortality

Did a heart catheter on the first day of intensive care raise mortality? A causal ML re-analysis of the SUPPORT study with 5,735 patients, compared with the randomized trials that later found no clear effect.

After adjusting for 50 day-1 covariates, a catheter goes with 3.5 percentage points higher 30-day mortality (95% CI 1.0 to 6.1), about half the raw difference of 7.4 points.

The size depends on the model: the doubly robust estimate with logistic models gives 6.8 pp, and only the causal forest lands inside the interval of the trials. In the linear sensitivity model, a hidden confounder 1.2 times as strong as all recorded diagnosis variables together would close the gap to the trials. The pipeline passes 4 of 6 robustness checks, and the two that fail are reported as such.

Data
SUPPORT study, 5,735 ICU patients, 2,184 with a catheter
Methods
Causal forest, doubly robust AIPW, overlap weights, Cinelli and Hazlett sensitivity analysis
Checks
Placebo treatment, random common cause, semi-synthetic outcome, trimming, nuisance models

Forecasting

Time-series forecasting pipeline

Monthly mortgage closures in a Swiss online-banking channel, predicted from interest-rate indicators and market factors for capacity planning.

A tuned Ridge model reaches R² 0.764 with an MAE of 3.29 closures per month. XGBoost reaches 0.617, SARIMAX 0.466.

Two customer channels turned out to behave differently. Modelling them separately gave more stable forecasts than a more complex single model. SHAP points to the SARON surcharge (lag 1) and the rent-price index (lag 3) as the strongest drivers. A static 80/20 split overstated performance, so the reported numbers use expanding-window validation.

Data
Confidential bank data. The repo ships synthetic data that runs the full pipeline.
Features
Five selection methods compared: Mutual Information, SelectKBest, RFE, Lasso, Random Forest
Tuning
Optuna, interpreted with SHAP and what-if scenarios

Causal inference

Smoking cessation and weight gain

How much weight do smokers gain because they quit? A causal ML pipeline on the NHEFS cohort, checked against the published estimate by Hernán and Robins.

Quitting causes 3.38 kg of additional weight gain over 11 years (95% CI 2.46 to 4.30). The effect passes 5 of 5 robustness checks.

The naive comparison understates the effect by about a quarter, because quitters were older and heavier at baseline. Four adjusted estimators land between 3.26 and 3.52 kg, and IPW gives 3.44 kg, matching the published 3.4 kg. An unmeasured confounder would need about 9 times the strength of age to erase the effect.

Data
NHEFS cohort, 1,566 smokers, 403 of them quit
Methods
Causal forest, IPW, AIPW, DAG with backdoor check, Cinelli and Hazlett sensitivity analysis

Deep learning

Image captioning on Flickr8k

Five captioning architectures on the same benchmark, from a CNN-LSTM baseline to a Transformer decoder with cross-attention.

Show, Attend and Tell reaches the best BLEU-4 (0.1915). Dot-product attention leads on METEOR (0.3961).

Attention raised BLEU-4 over the CNN-LSTM baseline (0.1863). The Transformer did not lead on 8,000 images, but it also trained for 20 epochs instead of 40, so the result is not a clean verdict on the architecture. The lowest perplexity (Show and Tell, 14.71) did not produce the best captions.

Data
Flickr8k, about 8,000 images with five reference captions each
Baseline
Pretrained ResNet-18 encoder with an LSTM decoder
Decoding
Greedy and beam search (k = 5)

Deep learning

CNN architecture study

Eight controlled image-classification experiments. Each one changes a single factor: depth, hyperparameters, regularization, optimizer or transfer learning.

A pretrained ResNet18 reaches 94.7% validation accuracy when fine-tuned and 94.5% as a feature extractor with only 5,130 trainable parameters. Trained from scratch, the same network reaches 77.7%.

The gap comes from the pretrained representations, not from the architecture. Fine-tuning peaked at epoch 7 and then began to overfit, while feature extraction kept the train-validation gap near zero. Adam and RMSprop converged clearly faster than SGD with or without momentum.

Data
Course-internal image dataset. The pipeline also runs on public sets such as CIFAR-10.
